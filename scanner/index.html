Innovator Journal: Spinning Scanner

What I Built

I built a simple spinning scanner. It has a ultrasonic distance sensor mounted to the top of the motor. As the motor moves, it turns the sensor slowly. At each movement the sensor is measuring how far away the motion is. The device includes a Ardunino Uno, Motor, board, Ultrasonic distance sensor, and a breadboard.

How I Built It

I built it by using different resources such as a Ardunino Uno, Motor, board, Ultrasonic distance sensor, and a breadboard. Also, me and a group of classamtes worked together to create this device.

What Was Interesting

Something I found interesting while watching and putting together this device is how the motor turns in tiny exact steps.
